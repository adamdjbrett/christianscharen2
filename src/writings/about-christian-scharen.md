# About Christian Scharen

A practical theologian, I was ordained a pastor in the ELCA in 2001.

## Education

- **Ph.D.** in Religion — Emory University

## Academic Work

My research and writing focuses on:
- Theology
- Worship and the arts
- Pastoral leadership
- Social justice
- Theological education

My early work on theological ethnography helped spark an international movement bringing together sociology and theology in the study of the church.

## Recent Publications

- *Ethnography as Christian Theology and Ethics: A Completely Revised 2nd Edition* (T&T Clark) — co-authored with Aana Vigen

## Current Projects

- **Decolonial theological memoir** about my family and the Ingalls of *The Little House on the Prairie* books
- **Book on Christian worship** before and after Christianity became the religion of Empire
