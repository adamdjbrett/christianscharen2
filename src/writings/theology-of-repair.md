# Theology of Repair: Settler Colonialism and Christian Faith

## The Problem

North American Christians are inheritors of a pioneer settler past. How might we work toward repair?

## The Path Forward

The intertwined stories of the Ingalls and Deloria families on lands near De Smet, South Dakota, offer theological grounding for:
- Speaking complicated truths
- Moving toward practical actions that repair relationships
