# Custer Died for Our Sins: A Practical Theology of Settler Repair

**Forthcoming Book**

How might North American Christian inheritors of a pioneer settler past work toward repair? Scharen traces the intertwined stories of the Ingalls and Deloria families on lands near De Smet, South Dakota, offering theological grounding for speaking complicated truths and resources for moving toward practical actions that repair relationships.
